FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to Common Questions

Clear details on privacy, licensing, offline performance, and AdSense readiness.

Is PixelCraft Studio 100% free to use?

Yes! All 11 tools are completely free with zero subscription fees, watermarks, or artificial limits. You can generate unlimited palettes, SVGs, waves, and shadows.

Are my images or private code uploaded to a server?

Never. PixelCraft is built on a 100% client-side zero-upload architecture. Image resizing, palette extraction, and SVG forge happen directly in your browser's memory using HTML5 Canvas & Web Workers.

Does it work offline on mobile and desktop?

Yes! PixelCraft is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Once loaded, our Network-First Service Worker caches assets so you can design even without an active internet connection.

Can I use the exported palettes and code commercially?

Absolutely. All color palettes, design tokens (CSS/Tailwind/JSON), SVGs, waves, shadows, and favicons you generate are yours to use in personal and commercial client projects with zero royalties.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Compliant with Google AdSense & GDPR Standards

1. Zero-Upload In-Browser Architecture

PixelCraft Studio operates entirely inside your web browser. When you upload photos for color extraction, image resizing, SVG inspection, or favicon generation, no files are ever uploaded or transmitted to any external server. All image processing, canvas quantization, and vector calculations take place strictly in your device's local memory.

2. LocalStorage & Cookies

We use standard HTML5 LocalStorage strictly to store your preferences (Dark/Light theme choice, sound effects toggle, saved palette presets in the Vault, and user feedback). These values remain on your local machine and are never sold or shared.

3. Google Identity Services (GIS) & Cloud Sync

If you choose to use the "Sign In with Google" feature, authentication is handled directly by Google's secure Identity Services (GIS). We only read your basic public profile information (Display Name, Email, and Avatar) to personalize your workspace session and sync your saved design tokens across devices. We never request access to private Google Drive files or personal account settings.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

This website may display advertisements powered by Google AdSense to support free server hosting. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

5. Contact & Data Inquiries

For any privacy questions or data deletion requests, you can contact the PixelCraft team through the in-app Feedback form or via our official GitHub repository.
